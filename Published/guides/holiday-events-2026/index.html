Have yourself a Florida little Christmas

There's no snow on the forecast, but Tampa Bay does the holidays its own way — Santa arriving by boat, light shows synced to music in Wesley Chapel, and Christmas trees you can cut down yourself in Dade City. I'm keeping track of the events my neighbors ask about every year and adding each one here as soon as its 2026 date is official.

Grab the cocoa, pick a few, and make some memories. See you out there!

xo, Jenn

Symphony in Lights Kickoff at The Shops at Wiregrass

Saturday, November 14, 2026 · 5 PM · Wesley Chapel, 28211 Paseo Dr

The season kicks off with Santa's arrival and the first show of hundreds of thousands of lights choreographed to holiday music, with "snowfall." After opening night, the light shows run nightly through the holidays — a perfect easy evening out from Land O' Lakes.

20

Winter Village Opens at Curtis Hixon Park

Friday, November 20, 2026 – Sunday, January 3, 2027 · Downtown Tampa, 600 N Ashley Dr

Real outdoor ice skating on the Riverwalk, plus local shops, a café, holiday lights and the Winter Village Express streetcar on select nights. Free to walk around; skating is ticketed — book your session online. Hours vary around the holidays.

Tampa's Santa Fest & Tree Lighting

Saturday, December 5, 2026 · Downtown Tampa, Curtis Hixon Waterfront Park

Parade through downtown at 1 PM, Santa Fest activities in the park from 1–5 PM, and the city tree lighting at 6 PM.

Tampa Riverwalk Holiday Lighted Boat Parade

Saturday, December 19, 2026 · 6:15–8:30 PM · Tampa Riverwalk

Tampa's largest lighted boat parade, winding up the Hillsborough River past Curtis Hixon Park. Thousands line the Riverwalk — arrive early.
