Events in Pasco County & Tampa Bay

Parades, lighted boats, light shows and Christmas on the farm — everything worth putting on the family calendar this season, from right here in Land O' Lakes to downtown Tampa.

Have yourself a Florida little Christmas

There's no snow on the forecast, but Tampa Bay does the holidays its own way — Santa arriving by boat, light shows synced to music in Wesley Chapel, and Christmas trees you can cut down yourself in Dade City. I'm keeping track of the events my neighbors ask about every year and adding each one here as soon as its 2026 date is official.

Grab the cocoa, pick a few, and make some memories. See you out there!

xo, Jenn
