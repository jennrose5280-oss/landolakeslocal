Events in Pasco & Tampa Bay

Parades, lighted boats, light shows and Christmas on the farm — everything worth putting on the family calendar this season, from right here in Land O' Lakes to downtown Tampa.

Have yourself a Florida little Christmas

There's no snow on the forecast, but Tampa Bay does the holidays its own way — Santa arriving by boat, light shows synced to music in Wesley Chapel, and Christmas trees you can cut down yourself in Dade City. I pulled together the events my neighbors ask about every year, starting close to home and working out from there.

Grab the cocoa, pick a few, and make some memories. See you out there!

xo, Jenn
About the dates: Events marked 2026 confirmed have an official 2026 date from the organizer. Everything else shows when it usually happens based on last season — organizers announce dates through November, and this page is updated as they do. Always check the official link before you head out.
