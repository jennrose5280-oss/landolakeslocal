Thanksgiving

in Land O' Lakes, Pasco & Tampa Bay

Turkey trots, dinner you don't have to cook, and ways to give back — everything for Thursday, Nov. 26, and the long weekend after.

A grateful kind of week

Whether you're running off the pie before you eat it, handing the cooking to someone else this year, or looking for a way to help a neighbor, here's what's happening around Land O' Lakes and Tampa Bay for Thanksgiving 2026. Every date here is confirmed for this year.

Thanksgiving morning races for runners, walkers and strollers. Two of the biggest are just up the road in Lutz and Wesley Chapel.

BayCare Wobble Turkey Trot

Thanksgiving morning · 7 AM · Tampa Premium Outlets, 2300 Grand Cypress Dr, Lutz

The closest trot to Land O' Lakes. Choose the 5K, the 1(Tur)K, a combo of both, or run virtually. Proceeds support the New Tampa Family YMCA. Packet pickup is Tue., Nov. 24 (4–7 PM, Fleet Feet Flatwoods), Wed., Nov. 25 (4–7 PM, outlets food court) or race morning 6–6:45 AM. Online registration closes at 7 PM on Nov. 25.

Wiregrass Turkey Trot

Thanksgiving morning · 7:15 AM · The Shops at Wiregrass, 28139 Paseo Dr, Wesley Chapel

The 14th annual trot, with a 5K and a 1-mile, hosted by the Rotary Club of New Tampa with AdventHealth Wesley Chapel. A big, family-friendly Wesley Chapel tradition that raises money for local charities.
